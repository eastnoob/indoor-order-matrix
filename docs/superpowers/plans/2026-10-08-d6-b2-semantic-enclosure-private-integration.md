## Concrete private request and failure contract

Use a small private request record or a validated mapping, whichever needs less code. Its required fields express the caller's AnalysisPlane, metre units, datum and cut relative to that plane, finite nonnegative contact distance in metres, finite nonnegative aperture area measurement budget in square metres, default-closed door/window scenario authority, and explicit authority for any task override of authored `AnalysisParticipation=exclude`. No geometry/measurement default may silently supply 1.5 m or 1e-6; these are explicit historical shadow experiment values only. Reject missing fields, booleans used as numbers, nonfinite/negative budgets and unsupported units/frames before computation. Datum/cut may be finite signed coordinates where the declared plane convention requires them. Keep the requested plane equal to the scene analysis plane for this integration; support for a second view frame is unnecessary.

Invalid requests raise a clear ValueError/TypeError rather than falling back to a guessed policy. Valid requests with missing/invalid/unsupported source geometry produce local refusal evidence and no carrier. Ordinary view calls perform no new computation and return the same signature, keys, values, provenance and domain/downstream refusals. Merely having source metadata does not invoke the private path.

Source roles/classes and authored SourceOpening relationships may identify eligible walls, columns, windows and doors; arbitrary property names cannot invent hosts or extents. Preserve authored identities, factual properties, source uncertainties and nullable adapter identities. Resolve all geometry_refs by ID, including multipart sources and missing references, rather than zipping sources to meshes. Already-world vertices receive no second transform. Preserve authored exclude while recording the specific task authority if overridden; no generic participation policy change. Furniture remains typed Analysis-only under its existing contract and contributes no enclosure support; beams contribute no support. Columns remain occupancy and separate structural support.

## Minimum geometry computation

Reuse production `mass_patch_from_mesh`, actual-face `plan_projection`, `component_shape` and the existing plane projection convention. The production height-band sampler is not an exact cut; port only the necessary pure indexed shared-edge exact section/topology qualification from the proven design logic into the private helper. Production imports must never depend on scripts/design, outputs, evidence JSON, oracle outputs or rendering. Treat component validity and source connectivity as distinct observations; do not accept invalid topology because a projection happened to be valid.

Compute exact physical sections independently. Localize potential wall apertures from actual source-face span projection minus qualified material section, without admitting the full wall projection as enclosure mass. Prefer consistent authored associations. Otherwise require one geometry-supported host/aperture, actual XY/depth locality and distinct jamb support; retain alternatives and refuse ambiguity. Explicit unfilled SourceOpening evidence vetoes a scenario seal; missing/conflicting authored references remain unresolved rather than selecting the nearest host. Surface span qualification is independent of volumetric watertightness: a thin/nonwatertight window or leaf absent at the cut can qualify its evidenced extent, while invalid/missing extent cannot.

Keep literal contact and contact within the caller's explicit distance budget distinguishable, with measured distances and evidence. No coordinate movement, global buffer/snap, hull/bbox closure, widened tolerance, blanket hole/gap repair or global context tolerance as hidden B2 policy. Close only a qualified local aperture using a separately labelled inferred/authored scenario carrier. Unknown, ambiguous and true unfilled apertures stay unsealed; numerical/local residuals retain their measured classification and local limitations.

Reuse component interior rings as provisional enclosure contours and filled actual component exterior as one provisional compound scope per wall-bearing carrier component with rings. Multiple contours may share one scope; do not allocate shared walls or certify rooms. Scope is not a bbox, empty interior, free-space or source mass subtraction. Preserve wall mass and interior-column occupancy. Output source observations, eligibility/authority, associations, carriers, contour/scope provenance and unresolved evidence only in the new private diagnostics child. Leave candidate selection/domain promotion/downstream evaluation refused.
