# Stage B fresh synthetic fixture contract

Task 1 generates fresh synthetic source-family inputs only. It does not fit a model, calibrate, score holdout predictions, or claim a gate result. The old pair replay and prior B1 datasets are not inputs.

Each of eight scenario strata contains six independently seeded source families: three development, one validation, and two holdout. A source family is the indivisible split unit. Each scene has six authored nodes, three external-skeleton candidates, no candidate with more than four targets, and no more than twelve targets total. The primary candidate universe is created from the scene skeleton before evidence generation.

The observations, truth, splits, and candidate-universe manifests are physically separate. The channels are continuous geometry, direction, clearance, and pattern residual; boolean semantic compatibility; and descriptive pattern conformity. Pattern conformity and residual share one measurement ID, so later inference must treat them as one measurement.

Source incidences explicitly associate source, node, and target reuse. Same measurement IDs are aliases of one measurement. Different measurements with a shared source incidence are correlated observations, not independent records. This fixture contract does not prescribe their statistical treatment.

The candidate contract retains overlap, nesting, singleton diagnostic, and uncovered-node labels. The observation-selected sensitivity route is explicitly not run; it does not claim a Stage A proposal comparison.
